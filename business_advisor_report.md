# Business Advisor Recommendations (2025-04-17)

Based on the financial report and sales activity summary as of 2025-04-17, here are 4 prioritized recommendations to improve revenue and operational efficiency for Beaver's Choice Paper Company:

1. Expand Product Assortment: Many declined customer requests were due to products not carried or non-standard sizes (e.g., A3, A5 paper sizes, balloons, recycled envelopes). Introducing a wider range of paper sizes and some popular niche products could capture unmet demand and increase sales.

2. Improve Order Fulfillment Timing: The most common reason for declined lines was inability to meet delivery timelines. Review and optimize supply chain and inventory management to reduce lead times and improve on-time availability, especially for high-demand items.

3. Review Discount Strategy: Discounts granted are significant ($1885.48 on $11,860.90 revenue). Analyze discount policies to ensure they are driving profitable sales and not eroding margins excessively. Consider targeted promotions on top sellers like Standard copy paper to boost volume without heavy discounting.

4. Maintain Inventory Levels: Currently, no stocked items are below minimum levels, which is positive. Continue monitoring stock levels closely to avoid stockouts and lost sales opportunities.

These recommendations are grounded in the data: unmet product variety demand, delivery timing issues, discount impact on revenue, and current inventory status.
